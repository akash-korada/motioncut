Spaghetti Bolognese

A classic Italian pasta dish with rich meat sauce.

  • Spaghetti
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onions
  • Garlic

1. Cook spaghetti.

2. Sauté onions and garlic.

3. Add beef and brown it.

4. Add sauce and simmer.

5. Combine and serve.

Vegetable Stir Fry

A quick and healthy mix of sautéed vegetables.

  • Bell peppers
  • Broccoli
  • Carrots
  • Soy sauce
  • Ginger

1. Chop vegetables.

2. Heat oil in a pan.

3. Stir fry veggies until tender.

4. Add soy sauce and ginger.

5. Serve hot.

Chole Bhature

A North Indian combo of spicy chickpeas and fried bread.

  • Chickpeas
  • Onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Garam masala
  • Flour (for Bhature)

1. Soak and boil chickpeas.

2. Make onion-tomato masala.

3. Add chickpeas and spices.

4. Knead dough and roll Bhature.

5. Fry and serve with chole.

Masala Dosa

A crispy South Indian dosa filled with spiced potato mix.

  • Dosa batter
  • Potatoes
  • Mustard seeds
  • Curry leaves
  • Green chilies

1. Cook potatoes and mash them.

2. Temper with spices.

3. Prepare dosa on hot tawa.

4. Add filling and fold.

5. Serve with chutney and sambar.

Paneer Butter Masala

Creamy and mildly spiced curry with paneer cubes.

  • Paneer
  • Tomatoes
  • Butter
  • Cashews
  • Cream

1. Blend tomato and cashew paste.

2. Cook with butter and spices.

3. Add paneer cubes.

4. Simmer with cream.

5. Serve hot with naan or rice.

Palak Paneer

Spinach-based curry with soft paneer cubes.

  • Spinach
  • Paneer
  • Garlic
  • Green chilies
  • Cumin seeds

1. Blanch and blend spinach.

2. Sauté garlic and spices.

3. Add spinach puree.

4. Add paneer and cook.

5. Serve warm with roti.
